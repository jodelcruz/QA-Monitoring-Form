Scoring Rule: The Actual Average Grade (%) is computed as Total Score ÷ (Sessions × 5) — the sum of all 5 evaluator scores across all sessions in the selected date range, divided by the number of sessions multiplied by 5 evaluators, expressed as a percentage out of 100. The grade is only computed once all 5 Candidate Support Team Members have submitted their evaluation for that proctor within the selected date range. Proctors with missing evaluations are marked as Incomplete.
